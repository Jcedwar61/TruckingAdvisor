## Natural Earth (map data)

Towns, US states, Canadian provinces, Mexican states, European countries and major highways
bundled in `src/TruckingAdvisor.Core/Mapping/Data/geo.json.gz` come from
[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) and are in the **public domain**.
Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.
The file is generated by `tools/build_gazetteer.py`.


## edge-tts (LGPL-3.0) — free neural voices

`src/TruckingAdvisor.EdgeTts` is a C# port of parts of [edge-tts](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts) 7.3.1 by rany2
and is licensed under the **GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0 or later** (`LICENSE.LGPL`, with `LICENSE.GPL`).
It is a separate library (`TruckingAdvisor.EdgeTts.dll`) used only through its public API. Its complete source and the
build scripts are in this repository, so you can modify it and rebuild TruckingAdvisor with your version. The voices
come from Microsoft's online read-aloud service used by Microsoft Edge (not an official API; needs internet).

## NLayer (MIT) — MP3 decoding

[NLayer](https://github.com/naudio/NLayer), MIT License, decodes the neural voices' MP3 audio.


## Whisper.net, whisper.cpp and Whisper models (MIT) — offline speech recognition

[Whisper.net](https://github.com/sandrohanea/whisper.net) (MIT, Copyright (c) 2024 sandrohanea) wraps
[whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) (MIT, Copyright (c) 2023-2024 The ggml authors), which ships
as `runtimes\win-x64\*.dll`. The English speech models (`ggml-small.en-q5_1.bin`, `ggml-base.en-q5_1.bin`) are
OpenAI's Whisper weights (MIT) converted by the whisper.cpp project; they are downloaded on first use from
huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp and checked against pinned SHA-256 checksums.
